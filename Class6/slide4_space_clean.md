```markdown
# Types of Cosmic Data 🚀

Understanding your interstellar data type guides visualization choice 🌌

1. **Point Data (Stellar Locations)**
   - **Definition:** Individual locations with celestial coordinates
   - **Examples:** Space station locations 🛰️, Astronaut addresses 🌠, Alien sightings 👽, Supernova epicenters 🌟, Communication satellites 📡
   - **Visualizations:** Scatter on star map, Cosmic bubble map, Galactic heat map, Nebula clustering

2. **Line Data (Orbits/Astrogation Paths)**
   - **Definition:** Connected sequences of stellar points
   - **Examples:** Orbital paths 🌌, Cosmic rivers 🌊, Starship flight paths 🚀, Delivery routes across galaxies 🌠, Interstellar travel lines
   - **Visualizations:** Orbit maps, Flow maps through the cosmos, Space network diagrams

3. **Polygon Data (Galactic Regions)**
   - **Definition:** Enclosed boundaries defining cosmic regions
   - **Examples:** Galaxies/constellations/nebulae 🌌, Cosmic ZIP codes 🌠, Trade territories in space 🚀, Star school districts 🌟, Cosmic climate zones 🌎
   - **Visualizations:** Cosmic choropleth maps, Space cartograms, Star boundary maps

4. **Raster Data (Galactic Grids)**
   - **Definition:** Continuous surfaces on celestial grids
   - **Examples:** Satellite imagery from space 🛰️, Galactic elevation DEMs 🗺️, Temperature surfaces of planets 🌡️, Space land cover, Precipitation across planets 🌧️
   - **Visualizations:** Galactic heat maps, Cosmic contour maps, 3D star surfaces 🌌
```
