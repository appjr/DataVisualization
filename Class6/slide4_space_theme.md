```markdown
# 🚀 Cosmic Cartography: Types of Galactic Data 🌌

*Charting the cosmos demands knowing your spatial data types for stellar visualizations*

## 🌟 1. Star Point Data (Discrete Cosmic Locations) ⭐

**Definition:** Celestial objects with specified coordinates in the universe

**Examples:**
- 🌟 Star locations
- 🪐 Planet positions
- 🛸 UFO sightings
- 🚀 Space probe landmarks
- 🛰️ Satellite spots

**Attributes:** May include data (brightness, type, discovery date, etc.)

**Visualizations:**
- Star scatter maps
- Bubble constellations (size = brightness)
- Heat nebulas (density of stars)
- Constellation clustering

## 🚀 2. Orbit Line Data (Interstellar Routes/Boundaries) 🌌

**Definition:** Cosmic highways connecting celestial points

**Examples:**
- 🚀 Spacecraft trajectories
- 🛰️ Satellite orbits
- 🌠 Comet paths
- 🌌 Cosmic strings
- 🌟 Asteroid belts

**Visualizations:**
- Orbit path maps
- Warp flow maps (thickness = traffic)
- Celestial network diagrams

## 🪐 3. Planet Polygon Data (Celestial Areas/Regions) 🌠

**Definition:** Encased boundaries defining stellar regions

**Examples:**
- ⭐ Galactic quadrants
- 🌌 Planetary territories
- 🌠 Star cluster zones
- 🪐 Astrobiology research regions
- 🌟 Nebula boundaries

**Visualizations:**
- Nebula color maps (color = energy levels)
- Cosmic cartograms (size = mass)
- Galactic boundary maps

## 💫 4. Cosmic Raster Data (Grid Surfaces) 🌟

**Definition:** Continuous cosmic surfaces on regular celestial grids

**Examples:**
- 🌌 Star field imagery
- 🌟 Cosmic microwave background maps
- ☄️ Temperature surface of planets
- 🌠 Galactic density grids
- 🪐 Mars topography

**Visualizations:**
- Cosmic heat maps
- Stellar contour maps
- 3D celestial surfaces
```
